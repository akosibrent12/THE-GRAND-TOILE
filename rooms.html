NEED TO KNOW

Frequently Asked Questions

Helpful information to make planning your stay easier and more convenient.

What time is check-in and check-out?

Standard check-in is at 3:00 PM, while check-out is at 12:00 PM. Early check-in and late check-out may be requested, subject to availability.

Can I request a specific room?

Yes. You may indicate your preferred room or accommodation when making a reservation. Requests are subject to availability.

Are children allowed in the resort?

Yes. Families are welcome. Please provide the number and ages of children when making your reservation so the appropriate accommodation can be arranged.

Can I cancel or modify my reservation?

Cancellation and modification policies depend on the selected booking arrangement. Please review your reservation terms or contact the reservations team for assistance.

Are the room rates inclusive of taxes?

Taxes, service charges, and other fees should be confirmed with the reservations team before completing your booking.

How can I make a reservation?

Visit our Reservations page and submit your preferred accommodation and stay details. Our reservations team can assist with your inquiry.
